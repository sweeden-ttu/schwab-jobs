1. In Texas, the “Texas Commercial Rules” (Special Requirements) apply to:
   A. Only out-of-state commercial drivers passing through Texas  
   B. Only hazardous materials drivers  
   C. All drivers of commercial motor vehicles operated on Texas highways  
   D. Only school bus drivers  
   Answer: C  
   Explanation: The Special Requirements section of the Texas CDL handbook applies to all commercial motor vehicles (CMVs) operating on Texas highways, not just certain endorsements.

2. In Texas, you must carry the current registration papers for your commercial vehicle:
   A. Only when driving outside city limits  
   B. Only if your vehicle is over 26,000 pounds GVWR  
   C. Any time the vehicle is operated on a public road  
   D. Only when hauling passengers  
   Answer: C  
   Explanation: Texas commercial rules require that registration papers be carried in the vehicle whenever it is operated on public roads.

3. A Texas commercial vehicle must stop at an open weigh station:
   A. Only if hauling hazardous materials  
   B. Only if over 10,000 pounds GVWR  
   C. Whenever directed by signs or law enforcement  
   D. Only if the driver is not on a tight schedule  
   Answer: C  
   Explanation: Texas law requires CMVs to enter and stop at open weigh stations when directed by signs or officials, regardless of load or schedule.

4. When must a Texas CMV have a current annual inspection certificate (sticker) displayed?
   A. Only if the vehicle is driven outside of Texas  
   B. Only if the vehicle is used for hire  
   C. Whenever the vehicle is operated on a Texas highway  
   D. Only when pulled over by law enforcement  
   Answer: C  
   Explanation: Texas commercial vehicles operated on state highways must have a current inspection and display proof as required.

5. In Texas, the maximum legal speed for a heavy truck or bus:
   A. Is always 55 mph statewide  
   B. May be lower than the posted car speed limit in some areas  
   C. Is always the same as the passenger car limit  
   D. Can be chosen by the driver based on conditions  
   Answer: B  
   Explanation: Some Texas highways have separate, lower speed limits for trucks and buses; CMV drivers must obey all special truck speed limits.

6. When must a Texas CMV driver stop at a railroad crossing, even if no train is visible?
   A. Only when carrying passengers  
   B. Only when carrying explosives or certain hazardous materials  
   C. Only when required by a stop sign at the tracks  
   D. Never, unless a train is approaching  
   Answer: B  
   Explanation: Texas rules and federal regulations require certain CMVs, such as those carrying specified hazardous materials, to stop at all railroad crossings.

7. The term “combination vehicle” under Texas commercial rules generally refers to:
   A. Any vehicle with more than two axles  
   B. A power unit (truck or tractor) towing one or more trailers  
   C. Any pickup truck with a camper shell  
   D. Only double- and triple-trailer rigs  
   Answer: B  
   Explanation: A combination vehicle consists of a power unit pulling one or more trailers or semitrailers.

8. Under Texas commercial rules, a vehicle that is NOT required to stop at a railroad crossing is:
   A. A vehicle carrying chlorine gas  
   B. A school bus with students on board  
   C. A truck hauling general freight with no placards  
   D. A vehicle carrying flammable liquids in bulk  
   Answer: C  
   Explanation: General freight vehicles with no placarded hazardous materials are usually not required to stop at all crossings, unless otherwise posted.

9. In Texas, a CMV must NOT be driven when its load:
   A. Extends more than one foot beyond the rear  
   B. Obscures the driver’s view to the front or sides  
   C. Contains any loose material  
   D. Weighs more than 5,000 pounds  
   Answer: B  
   Explanation: Commercial rules prohibit loads that block the driver’s safe view to the front or sides or interfere with control of the vehicle.

10. When must a Texas CMV display red flags or lights on a projecting load?
    A. Whenever any cargo extends past the rear bumper  
    B. Only at night if the load extends four feet or more beyond the rear  
    C. When the load extends more than four feet beyond the rear or more than two feet beyond the sides  
    D. Only when hauling pipe or poles  
    Answer: C  
    Explanation: Special requirements require visible warning flags in daylight and lights at night when cargo projects beyond specified distances.

11. Texas rules require mud flaps or splash guards on most commercial vehicles to:
    A. Improve fuel economy  
    B. Keep the vehicle cleaner  
    C. Reduce spray and protect other road users from debris  
    D. Support the rear bumper  
    Answer: C  
    Explanation: Splash guards are required to help control mud, water, and debris thrown by the tires.

12. A CMV that is “out of service” under Texas or federal rules:
    A. May be driven to the next delivery point if close by  
    B. Must not be driven until the defect is corrected  
    C. May operate only during daylight hours  
    D. Can operate only without cargo  
    Answer: B  
    Explanation: A vehicle placed out of service for safety defects cannot be driven again until repairs are completed.

13. Which of the following is TRUE about seat belt use under Texas commercial rules?
    A. Only required on interstate highways  
    B. Only required when carrying passengers  
    C. Required for the driver whenever the vehicle is in motion  
    D. Optional for experienced drivers  
    Answer: C  
    Explanation: CMV drivers must use seat belts whenever the vehicle is in motion, regardless of road type or load.

14. Texas commercial rules require that reflective triangles or flares be carried on most CMVs so that:
    A. The vehicle can be legally parked overnight  
    B. They can be used to mark a stopped vehicle on the roadway or shoulder  
    C. The driver can signal for help in a city  
    D. They can be used as wheel chocks  
    Answer: B  
    Explanation: Warning devices are required emergency equipment for marking a stopped or disabled commercial vehicle.

15. When must a Texas CMV driver use headlights, even in the daytime?
    A. When visibility is less than 1,000 feet  
    B. Whenever using windshield wipers  
    C. Only in construction zones  
    D. Only at dawn and dusk  
    Answer: A  
    Explanation: Texas law requires headlights when visibility is seriously reduced (for example, less than about 1,000 feet) due to weather or conditions.

16. Under Texas rules, who is responsible for making sure a commercial vehicle is not overloaded and is properly secured?
    A. Only the shipper  
    B. Only the company safety officer  
    C. The driver and, in many cases, the motor carrier  
    D. Only the dock personnel  
    Answer: C  
    Explanation: The driver shares responsibility with the carrier to ensure the vehicle is within legal weight limits and the load is properly secured.

17. A Texas CMV must NOT be operated on a highway if:
    A. It has more than five axles  
    B. Any tire has exposed fabric or cord  
    C. It has a diesel engine  
    D. The fuel tank is more than half full  
    Answer: B  
    Explanation: Tires with exposed fabric or cord are unsafe and violate inspection and safety requirements.

18. In Texas, when a CMV is required to display hazardous materials placards, the placards must be:
    A. Placed only on the rear of the vehicle  
    B. Placed on the right side and rear only  
    C. Placed on all four sides of the vehicle  
    D. Optional if carrying less than a full load  
    Answer: C  
    Explanation: Hazardous materials placards must be visible on all four sides of the vehicle when required.

19. Texas commercial rules require that brakes on a CMV:
    A. Be used only on hills  
    B. Be able to stop and hold the vehicle safely on any grade it is operated  
    C. Be adjusted only by a mechanic  
    D. Be inspected only once a year  
    Answer: B  
    Explanation: Brakes must safely control and hold the vehicle on the grades on which it is operated.

20. Which statement about Texas commercial vehicle lighting is TRUE?
    A. Clearance lamps are required on most wide or long CMVs  
    B. Only one red tail lamp is required on any CMV  
    C. CMVs do not need reflectors if they have working lights  
    D. Marker lights are only required on trucks over 80,000 pounds GVWR  
    Answer: A  
    Explanation: Many CMVs must have clearance and marker lamps and reflectors to indicate their size at night or in low visibility.

21. Under Texas commercial rules, a CMV must NOT be operated when the load is:
    A. Properly covered and secured  
    B. Leaking, spilling, or blowing off the vehicle  
    C. Evenly distributed over the axles  
    D. Within legal weight and size limits  
    Answer: B  
    Explanation: It is illegal and unsafe to operate a CMV if the load is dropping, leaking, sifting, or blowing from the vehicle.

22. A Texas CMV driver who is involved in a collision on a public highway must:
    A. Move the vehicle immediately, even if unsafe  
    B. Only exchange insurance information  
    C. Stop, render aid if possible, and report the crash as required by law  
    D. Continue to the next exit and call from there  
    Answer: C  
    Explanation: Texas law requires drivers involved in crashes to stop, give aid when possible, and make any required reports.
